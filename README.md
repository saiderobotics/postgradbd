### 🌟 Post Graduation Bangladesh

Here’s why this site is an essential tool for anyone seeking a Master's, MPhil, or PhD in Bangladesh:

1.  **Comprehensive and Centralized Database**
    - Instead of browsing dozens of individual university websites, you get a single, unified list of postgraduate programs from both **public and private universities** across Bangladesh. This saves significant time and effort.

2.  **Powerful Multi-Criteria Filtering**
    - The site allows you to filter programs based on your specific needs:
        - **By Degree:** Instantly narrow down to only `MSc`, `MPhil`, or `PhD` programs.
        - **By University Type:** Easily switch between viewing only `Public` or `Private` universities.
        - **By Location (Division):** Find programs in your preferred region (e.g., Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi). This is perfect for those looking to study close to home or in a specific city.

3.  **Real-Time Search Functionality**
    - The live search bar lets you quickly type any keyword—be it a university name, a field of study (like "Engineering"), or even a division—and the list updates instantly. This makes finding specific information incredibly fast.

4.  **Clear and Intuitive Information Display**
    - Each program card is designed for clarity, showing the **University Name**, **Field of Study**, **University Type** (Public/Private), **Degree Type** (MSc/MPhil/PhD), and **Location** at a glance. This helps you compare options quickly without reading through walls of text.

5.  **Up-to-Date and User-Friendly Interface**
    - The site features a clean, modern design with a prominent hero section that provides a quick summary of available data (total programs and universities). It also includes a timestamp, giving you confidence that the information is fresh.

6.  **Direct Access to Official Information**
    - The "Details" button on each card acts as a launchpad to the official university website or the specific program page, allowing you to check the most current admission requirements, deadlines, and application procedures directly from the source.
